Spool Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Spool is made by David Defranceski. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

Spool works on your device. It does not collect, store or share your personal information. We run no servers, have no accounts, and use no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Your photos and videos

Spool asks for access to your Photos library so it can show your Live Photos, videos and photos and build movies from them. It only reads them. It never edits or deletes them. Everything it makes (drafts, edits and finished movies) is stored privately on your device, inside the app. Your photos and videos are not uploaded anywhere by Spool.

If you tap "Save to Photos", Spool adds the finished movie or GIF to your Photos library. If you choose Share, the movie goes where you send it, using your device's share sheet.

Location

If a photo has a location saved with it and you turn on "Place" on a clip, or use Quick movie with Places switched on, Spool shows the place name, for example "Cape Byron". To find the name, your device sends that photo's coordinates to Apple's location service, as any app does when it looks up a place. Spool does not receive, keep or send those coordinates anywhere else. Switch off Places to avoid the lookup.

Face and image analysis

When you use Quick movie, Spool analyses your clips on your device (sharpness, light, faces, and whether someone is talking) to pick the best ones. This runs entirely on your device. Nothing is sent from it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal data.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share data, because we do not have any.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising.

Crash reports

If you install Spool through TestFlight or the App Store, Apple may give the developer anonymous crash reports if you have chosen to share them with developers in your device settings. They contain no photos.

Children

Spool is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new date.
